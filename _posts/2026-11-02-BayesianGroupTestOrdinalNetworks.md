Network models are increasingly used to model multivariate data and many research questions
are about whether networks differ across groups. This tutorial provides an accessible introduction
to Bayesian model-selection for testing group differences in networks, as implemented in the R
package [bgms](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/bgms/index.html). This approach provides posterior inclusion probabilities and Bayes factors for each
group difference, allowing researchers to distinguish evidence for a difference from evidence for
equality.  We focus the tutorial on group differences in ordinal network models, since the vast
majority of data in cross-sectional research are measured on ordinal scales. We explain the model
parameters, prior choices, missing-data handling, convergence checks, checks of model fit, and
the interpretation of posterior summaries of group differences. The workflow is illustrated with a
reproducible comparison of problematic pornography use symptom networks in women and men The preprint is available [here](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/rx745_v1).
